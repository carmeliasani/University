## A Design Thinking Case Study

As part of my SECP1513 coursework, I worked with my team to design CareSense—a digital solution for the ADHD community. We combined a wearable sweat‑sensing patch with a mobile app to help caregivers detect early signs of overstimulation and provide timely support. This project sharpened my design thinking skills, especially in empathizing with users and iterating toward practical, human‑centered innovation. Our journey from research to prototype is fully documented in the attached report.
